## Publication record rules

Every work must have a stable ID, title, editorial category, programme, publication type, status, author, created/published/revised dates, abstract, research question, sources, method, limitations, canonical Corpus path and related work.

Do not assign a publication date before publication. Do not imply peer review, completed research, community approval or independent verification without evidence. The website displays only actual, approved works.

## Publication workflow

1. QUESTION / EXPLORATION — establish the inquiry.
2. WORKING THOUGHT / REFLECTION — develop and test an idea.
3. RESEARCH — document sources, methods, claims and limitations.
4. POSITION / ARGUMENT — state and defend the author's position.
5. EDITORIAL REVIEW — verify attribution, evidence, context and publication permissions.
6. PUBLISHED — record actual publication date and canonical path.
7. REVISED / RETRACTED — preserve revision and correction history.

The website's six editorial categories and the eight research programmes are separate classifications. A work may be cross-listed, but it has one canonical Corpus record.
