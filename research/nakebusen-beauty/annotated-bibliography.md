**Editorial rule:** Abstracts and indexed extracts are discovery aids. Where the full text and methods have not been reviewed, that limitation is stated. Record access date, exact page/section, and quotation only after direct inspection.

## 1. Natural hair, identity, and Kenya

### 1.1 Edna G. Ndichu and Shikha Upadhyaya — “Going natural: Black women’s identity project shifts in hair care practices”

- **Publication:** *Consumption Markets & Culture*, vol. 22, issue 1, pp. 44–67; published online 26 April 2018.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1080/10253866.2018.1456427
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed journal article.
- **Relevance:** Directly investigates urban Black women in Kenya and the meanings associated with moving away from hair-texture alteration and adopting natural hair practices.
- **Supports:** Treating hair care as a potentially meaningful identity and consumption practice in a Kenyan context.
- **Does not establish:** Current Eldoret customer preferences, loc-specific market size, a universal meaning of natural hair, or the health superiority of every natural-hair practice.
- **Review status:** Abstract/metadata inspected; full text and methods need detailed review.
- **Next action:** Obtain accessible full text; extract research questions, recruitment, sample, methods, findings, limitations, and quotations with page numbers.

## 2. Beauty work, platforms, and Kenya’s informal economy

### 2.1 International Labour Organization / Thunderbird Africa — *Digital Labour in Kenya*

- **Publication:** Report PDF, 2024 publication.
- **URL:** https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/ILOP-Digital-Labour-Kenya-v6_0.pdf
- **Type:** Research report.
- **Relevant content:** Sections examining beauty workers in traditional settings and on platforms. The report describes a survey of 25 workers in the traditional beauty sector and 54 working via beauty and social-media platforms.
- **Relevance:** Offers a concrete starting point for understanding how beauty practitioners use platforms and the working conditions associated with digital channels.
- **Critical limitation:** The report says these small samples are not representative of the broader traditional or platform beauty workforce. Do not present the sample as a national estimate.
- **Review status:** Relevant indexed text inspected; complete section and methodology review remains necessary.
- **Next action:** Review the full beauty-work chapter and note survey dates, recruitment, sample composition, definitions, and any reported limits.

### 2.2 International Labour Organization — *Pathways to Market Inclusion for Urban Refugees and Host Communities*

- **Date:** 2 December 2025.
- **URL:** https://www.ilo.org/publications/pathways-market-inclusion-urban-refugees-and-host-communities
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.54394/MSZN0513
- **Type:** Market-systems analysis.
- **Scope:** Beauty and personal care, creative, and construction sectors in Nairobi and Mombasa, with a focus on market inclusion for urban refugees and host communities.
- **Relevance:** Useful for a framework of market access, enterprise constraints, inclusion, skills, and decent work.
- **Limitations:** Not a loc-care market study and not an Eldoret market-size estimate. Its focus population and geography must be kept explicit.
- **Review status:** Publication page and abstract inspected; full report review required before using detailed findings.
- **Next action:** Extract only findings relevant to beauty/personal care, clearly labelled by geography and target population.

### 2.3 International Labour Organization — *The Informal Economy in Kenya*

- **Date:** 20 September 2021.
- **URL:** https://www.ilo.org/publications/informal-economy-kenya
- **Type:** Country-level informal-economy assessment.
- **Relevance:** Background on informal enterprise, employment, remuneration, formalisation constraints, and business support.
- **Limitations:** Older than current market conditions; not specific to loc services.
- **Review status:** Metadata/abstract inspected.
- **Next action:** Use for structural context only and compare with newer official Kenyan data.

### 2.4 Kenya Labour Market Information System — Informal Sector Skills Survey

- **URL:** https://labourmarket.go.ke/informal-sector-skills/
- **Type:** Official labour-market information portal.
- **Relevance:** Discovery point for Kenya informal-sector occupations, skills, earnings, and employment indicators.
- **Limitations:** Indicators need to be inspected for date, definitions, occupation classification, and coverage. Do not assume all hair or beauty work is separately represented.
- **Review status:** Portal inspected.
- **Next action:** Record relevant tables, metadata, survey years, and downloadable source files before quoting statistics.

## 3. Hair discrimination, wellbeing, and comparative research

### 3.1 Manka Nkimbeng et al. — “The Person Beneath the Hair: Hair Discrimination, Health, and Well-Being”

- **Publication:** *Health Equity*, first published online 2 August 2023.
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10457631/
- **Type:** Open-access scholarly commentary/review.
- **Relevance:** Discusses hair discrimination and possible implications for health and wellbeing.
- **Supports:** Treating hair-related stigma as a legitimate research and client-respect issue.
- **Limitations:** Much of the legal and social context discussed is US-centred. It does not estimate prevalence in Kenya or establish Kenyan legal remedies.
- **Review status:** Article landing page and abstract inspected; full-text review recommended before detailed paraphrase.
- **Next action:** Separate the article’s cited empirical findings from commentary and US-specific policy context.

### 3.2 CROWN Workplace Research Studies

- **URL:** https://www.thecrownact.com/research-studies
- **Type:** Advocacy-linked research resource.
- **Relevance:** Workplace hair bias in the United States.
- **Limitations:** Geography and sponsoring organisation matter; study methods and questionnaires must be checked. Do not transfer percentages to Kenya.
- **Review status:** Discovery source only.
- **Next action:** Use only as comparative context and seek Kenyan-specific evidence.

## 5. Hair-care market data

### 5.1 Euromonitor International — *Hair Care in Kenya*

- **URL:** https://www.euromonitor.com/hair-care-in-kenya/report
- **Type:** Commercial market research report; paid access.
- **Relevance:** National retail hair-care categories, manufacturers, trends, and forecasts.
- **Limitations:** Retail hair-product sales are not equivalent to salon service revenue, loc-care demand, or NAKEBUSEN’s addressable market. A paid abstract is not enough to audit detailed estimates.
- **Review status:** Product/report listing inspected; full report not reviewed.
- **Next action:** Do not cite detailed market-size figures without access to the report methodology, definitions, base year, and data tables. For Eldoret service demand, conduct local primary research.

2. Review the ILO beauty-work sections and retain the sample-size caveat in every use.
