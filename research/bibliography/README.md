# FELOH Corpus — Research bibliography

**Status:** LIVE scaffold · Version 0.1 · 25 October 2026  
**Governing standards:** [EVIDENCE-AND-EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md](../../EVIDENCE-AND-EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) · [EVIDENCE-AND-PROVENANCE-METHOD.md](../../EVIDENCE-AND-PROVENANCE-METHOD.md)

This directory is the **source register** for the FELOH Corpus and *Sun Burn — Black Musings*: a traceable inventory of original writing, conversations, historical records, scholarship, oral histories, datasets, technical documentation and other research materials.

**Purpose:** Not merely to collect links. Every entry must explain what a source contains, what it can substantiate, reliability for a particular claim, and relation to the corpus.

## Governing rules (summary)

- Source-first; evidence before interpretation
- Preserve originals; no silent rewrite
- Provenance recorded; no invented completeness
- Discovery ≠ authentication ≠ publication approval
- Consent, privacy, cultural protocols for living contributors

English is the main documentation language; Kiswahili annotations welcome where useful for Kenyan audiences.
