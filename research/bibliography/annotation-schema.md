# Master bibliography annotation schema

One record per distinct source or edition. Complete every field that applies; mark **UNKNOWN** when missing rather than inventing.

## Identification

- Unique source ID (e.g. `MB-ARCH-001`, `MB-FEL-001`)
- Full title and subtitle
- Creator, author, interviewer or institution
- Creation date and publication date (separately)
- Source type and language
- Edition, version, DOI, ISBN, archive identifier or stable URL

## Provenance and access

- Original repository or custodian
- Original file, scan, recording or physical location
- Date accessed or acquired
- Original versus derivative status
- File checksum and preservation location, where applicable
- Access restrictions, licence, copyright and consent status

## Annotation

- Neutral description of contents
- Research question(s) the source addresses
- Key claims or passages (exact page, section or timestamp)
- Evidence type and method used by the creator
- Supporting and conflicting sources
- Strengths, limitations, omissions and potential bias
- Relevance to *Sun Burn — Black Musings* and the wider FELOH Corpus

## Disposition

- Verification status (see evidence-labels.md)
- Confidence and unresolved uncertainty
- Related claim IDs and bibliography IDs
- Researcher or annotator and annotation date
- Inclusion, restriction or exclusion rationale
- Publication status and authorised reuse status
