## Current research programme: Before Africa

The curated, annotated source register is maintained at [docs/research-briefs/before-africa-source-catalogue-2026-11.md](../docs/research-briefs/before-africa-source-catalogue-2026-11.md). It includes primary studies, institutional resources, supplementary-data guidance, source metadata requirements and an acquisition queue. The master programme framework is [the Before Africa dossier](../docs/before-africa-research-dossier-2026-11-07.md); the regional synthesis is [the continental atlas and populations brief](../docs/research-briefs/continental-atlas-and-populations-2026-11.md).

This catalogue is a curated starting point. Future source records should also be accessioned into the bibliography and claims registers as those structured records are populated. Do not copy restricted genomic datasets, sensitive site coordinates or community-held knowledge into public files without authority.
