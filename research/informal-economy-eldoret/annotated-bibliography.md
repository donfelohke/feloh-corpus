# Annotated bibliography — Informal Economy in Eldoret and Uasin Gishu

**Status:** RESEARCH · initial source set  
**Reviewed:** 25 October 2026  
**Scope:** Kenya national evidence and Uasin Gishu local planning  
**Rule:** Sources are not interchangeable. Distinguish national statistics, legal/policy frameworks, county plans, and independent analysis.

## 1. Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS). *Economic Survey 2026*.

- **Type:** Official national statistical publication.
- **URL:** https://www.knbs.or.ke/reports/2026-economic-survey/
- **Relevant material:** Employment chapter and accompanying tables; national recorded employment and estimated informal-sector employment for 2021–2025.
- **Supported claim:** The report records estimated informal-sector employment of 18.1061 million in 2025, out of 21.6007 million total recorded employment. It states that informal-sector employment accounted for 87.2% of the 822.1 thousand increase in recorded employment in 2025.
- **Scope and method:** National annual economic survey; the reported total excludes rural small-scale farming and pastoral activities. The 2025 figure is provisional and the informal-sector number is estimated.
- **Limitations:** Not a local Eldoret estimate; does not count every type of informal economic activity; cannot be used alone to infer enterprise counts, earnings, working conditions or causal explanations.
- **Use:** National context and benchmark only.
- **Review status:** Official publication page and PDF search result checked 25 October 2026.
- **Citation URL:** https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-Economic-Survey.pdf

## 2. International Labour Organization (ILO). *Recommendation No. 204 concerning the transition from the informal to the formal economy* (2015).

- **Type:** International labour standard; recommendation, not a Kenya-specific statistical report.
- **URL:** https://www.ilo.org/dyn/normlex/en/f?p=NORMLEXPUB:12100:0::NO::P12100_INSTRUMENT_ID:3243110
- **Relevant material:** Objectives, scope and definition of the informal economy; workers and economic units; rights, livelihoods and enterprise sustainability.
- **Supported claim:** The ILO frames the informal economy in terms of activities not covered or insufficiently covered by formal arrangements and calls for transitions that respect fundamental rights and support income security, livelihoods and entrepreneurship.
- **Limitations:** Normative guidance; not evidence that a policy is implemented or that a particular intervention works in Uasin Gishu.
- **Use:** Working definition, rights-based framework and policy evaluation criteria.
- **Review status:** Official ILO NORMLEX text checked 25 October 2026.

## 3. ILO. *The Informal Economy in Kenya*.

- **Type:** Country-focused research report.
- **URL:** https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/@ed_emp/@emp_ent/documents/publication/wcms_820312.pdf
- **Relevant material:** Definitions, measurement challenges and Kenya-specific discussion of informal-sector units and employment.
- **Contribution:** Explains why “informal economy,” “informal employment,” “microenterprise,” and “jua kali” should not be used as exact synonyms, and documents definitional and measurement challenges in Kenya.
- **Limitations:** Check publication date, underlying data periods, authorship, references and methods in the full report before using any numerical estimate as current. The report is not a substitute for newer KNBS data.
- **Use:** Conceptual and historical context; trace cited claims back to the original data source where possible.
- **Review status:** Official ILO-hosted PDF and search extract checked 25 October 2026; detailed page-by-page extraction remains outstanding.

## 4. County Government of Uasin Gishu. *County Integrated Development Plan (CIDP) 2023–2027*.

- **Type:** County planning document.
- **Official landing page:** https://uasingishu.go.ke/index.php/download/county-integrated-development-plan-cidp-2023-2027
- **Repository copy and metadata:** https://repository.kippra.or.ke/handle/123456789/4692
- **Relevant material:** Trade and industrialisation, local economic development, employment, markets, infrastructure, youth and women’s participation, programme targets and county budgeting.
- **Contribution:** Establishes the county's stated plans, priorities and indicators against which implementation evidence can later be compared.
- **Limitations:** A plan is not proof of delivery or impact. Extract exact page/table numbers, baseline year, target year, responsible department and indicator definition before citing individual commitments.
- **Use:** Local policy baseline and interview prompts for county departments, trader groups and market managers.
- **Review status:** Official county landing page and repository record checked 25 October 2026; full PDF extraction and target verification remain outstanding.

## 5. County Government of Uasin Gishu / Eldoret City. *Uasin Gishu County Urban Institutional Development Strategy 2022–2027*.

- **Type:** Urban development strategy.
- **URL:** https://eldoretcity.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/CUIDS-REPORT-2022_2027.pdf
- **Relevant material:** Urban infrastructure, markets, transport and traffic, public space and economic development priorities.
- **Contribution:** Provides urban-planning context for identifying where economic activity intersects with transport nodes, market infrastructure and public-space management.
- **Limitations:** Strategy statements and project listings require independent status checks; recommendations are not evidence of completion, present-day conditions or outcomes.
- **Use:** Guide site-mapping questions and identify records to request, not to assume where all current informal activity occurs.
- **Review status:** Public PDF link and search extract checked 25 October 2026; full document and project-status verification remain outstanding.

## 6. KNBS. *2026 Kenya Integrated Labour Force Survey (ILFS)*.

- **Type:** Official survey programme page.
- **URL:** https://www.knbs.or.ke/2026-kenya-integrated-labour-force-survey-ilfs/
- **Page date:** 26 June 2026.
- **Relevant material:** Survey purpose and planned coverage, including informal employment and digital-platform employment.
- **Contribution:** Signals an official source to monitor for updated labour-market measures and methodological documentation.
- **Limitations:** A survey announcement or FAQ is not a published findings dataset. Do not cite final estimates until the report, tables, definitions, reference period and release date are available and checked.
- **Use:** Monitor for results, questionnaire, technical report and microdata access conditions.
- **Review status:** Official KNBS page checked 25 October 2026.

## 7. County Government of Uasin Gishu. Trade, Industrialization, Cooperatives and Enterprise Development service page.

- **Type:** Official county department/service page.
- **URL:** https://uasingishu.go.ke/index.php/service/tradeindustrialization-cooperatives-and-enterprise-development
- **Relevant material:** Publicly stated commitments about trader conditions, licensing, stalls, markets, SACCOs and a county economic forum.
- **Contribution:** Helps formulate specific, verifiable questions about services and policy delivery.
- **Limitations:** Web copy is a statement of priorities, not proof of implementation, budget allocation, access, or impact. Verify each claim with dated budgets, programme reports, procurement/implementation records and participant evidence.
- **Use:** Build a policy implementation verification table.
- **Review status:** Official county page checked 25 October 2026.
