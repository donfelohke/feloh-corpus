This is a proposed collection entry point. The title is based on the collection name supplied for research; no assumption is made about its final title, scope, chronology, or publication intent.

## Collection purpose

Provide a traceable home for original writing associated with *Sun Burn — Black Musings*, if and when source material is located and its inclusion is confirmed.

The collection may eventually contain essays, poems, fragments, notebooks, thematic indexes, chronology, source notes, and editorial history. Those categories are proposals, not evidence that such materials currently exist.

## Intake rules

- Do not create placeholder works that could be mistaken for real authored pieces.
- Do not reconstruct missing text from memory or AI inference and present it as verbatim.
- Keep the original source unchanged; put edited or formatted versions in separately identified files.
- Record exact source references and dates when available.
- Mark uncertain title, date, authorship, version, and publication permission as `UNKNOWN` or `UNREVIEWED`.
- Separate the author's words from editorial or AI commentary.
- Do not publish personal or private material without explicit approval for the intended audience.

## Proposed collection structure

Create these directories only as actual material and editorial needs arise:

- `works/` — verified source works and clearly labeled editions.
- `themes/` — thematic index with references to works.
- `chronology/` — dated entries supported by source evidence.
- `sources/` — bibliography and provenance notes.
- `editorial/` — revision history and editorial policy.

## First milestone

Build a source inventory before importing content. Search the accessible repository, conversation files, and user-designated source locations; report which sources were searched and what remains inaccessible. Confirm collection scope and publication intent with the author before public release.
