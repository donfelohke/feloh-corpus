# Brand Constitution — GRACE · GRIT · GLORY

**Status:** CANONICAL BRAND DIRECTION  
**Scope:** Shared brand meaning, public signature, and audience-specific deployment  
**Applies to:** NAKEBUSEN, WARENA, DON FELOH / #HisGenjeness  
**Change control:** Review through a focused pull request; do not silently redefine this constitution in specialist repositories.

## 1. Unified signature

**NAKEBUSEN · WARENA · DON FELOH**  
**GRACE · GRIT · GLORY**  
**HERITAGE · BLACK EXCELLENCE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES**

The three entities remain distinct and are presented in this order. The umbrella statement is not a fourth pillar.

> Grace grounds us. Grit builds us. Glory expresses what we become.

## 2. The three pillars

### GRACE — NAKEBUSEN

**Beauty · Heritage · Craft · Human dignity**

NAKEBUSEN is the human-facing beauty, care, craft, livelihood, and heritage domain. Grace is excellence experienced through attentive service, cultural continuity, care, and dignity.

### GRIT — WARENA

**Technology · Infrastructure · Freedom · Capability**

WARENA is the computing, systems, infrastructure, and technical-capability domain. Grit is the disciplined work that makes capability durable: reproducibility, maintainability, repair, privacy, and freedom from unnecessary dependency.

### GLORY — DON FELOH / #HisGenjeness

**Authorship · Embodiment · Creative vision · Achievement**

DON FELOH (#HisGenjeness) is the human authorship and creative-expression position. Glory means making work, practice, and possibility visible; it is not a claim of superiority or a substitute for evidence of achievement.

Public creative signature: **#HisGenjeness** · **@donfelohke** · Felix Lwova.

## 3. Public-facing enterprise: NAKEBUSEN · LOCS & DIGITAL

**One identity. Two practices. One human author.**

NAKEBUSEN is the public-facing home for both commercial practices. WARENA powers the digital practice. #HisGenjeness expresses the human creative identity behind the work.

| Practice | Pillar language | Public line |
|----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **NAKEBUSEN · LOCS** | GRACE · ROOT · CROWN | Natural loc care, retwists, retightening and styling. Beauty as identity, confidence, craft and freedom. **Your Beauty. Your Way.** |
| **NAKEBUSEN · DIGITAL** | GRIT · SYSTEMS · CAPABILITY | Technology services, GNU/Linux, systems engineering, development environments, digital products and infrastructure. **Powered by WARENA. Freedom Is a System.** |
| **#HisGenjeness** | GLORY · AUTHORSHIP · EXPRESSION | Human creative identity connecting locs, technology, live coding, Genge, choreohacking and cultural expression. |

### Essential distinction

- **NAKEBUSEN** — public-facing home for **both** LOCS and DIGITAL services  
- **WARENA** — enables / powers the digital practice (not a separate consumer checkout brand)  
- **#HisGenjeness** — human creative identity (author, craftsperson, engineer, artist)  
- **GENJE** embodies · **IKAI** reflects (research/creative layers; not checkout brands)

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.**

## 4. Constitutional relationship

The pillars are complementary, not interchangeable:

- **NAKEBUSEN / GRACE** grounds the ecosystem in human relationships, service, craft, and dignity (including LOCS and the commercial face of DIGITAL).
- **WARENA / GRIT** builds and maintains the systems and capabilities that support DIGITAL and technical work.
- **DON FELOH / GLORY / #HisGenjeness** authors and expresses creative work made possible through practice and capability.

The sequence is a brand and meaning-making framework. It does not by itself prove that any proposed technical system, product, or research project has been implemented or validated.

## 5. Audience-specific signature variants

The primary signature above is the default for broad brand use. The second line may be adapted to the context without changing the three-pillar mapping.

### Global African-diaspora, cultural, and creative contexts

**HERITAGE · BLACK EXCELLENCE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES**

### Kenyan cooperative, institutional, and public-sector contexts

**HERITAGE · SOVEREIGNTY · FUTURES**  
Alternative: **HERITAGE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES · COOPERATIVE FUTURES**

Choose wording for the actual audience. These are deployment variants, not replacements for the three pillars.

## 6. Cultural and historical grounding

The constitution is intended to support African self-representation, cultural continuity, technical self-determination, and futures shaped by African authorship. Relevant cultural, historical, philosophical, and institutional references should be documented with accurate citations and context.

Do not treat analogies between GRACE / GRIT / GLORY and a historical programme, philosophical tradition, or research framework as proof that those sources endorse this brand architecture.

## 7. Deployment rules

1. Keep the order **NAKEBUSEN · WARENA · DON FELOH** unless a documented, context-specific design decision requires otherwise.
2. Keep the mapping **GRACE—NAKEBUSEN; GRIT—WARENA; GLORY—DON FELOH / #HisGenjeness** stable across official materials.
3. Present **LOCS** and **DIGITAL** as two practices under **NAKEBUSEN**, not as separate transacting brands.
4. Treat **HERITAGE · BLACK EXCELLENCE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES** as the primary umbrella statement, with audience-specific alternatives in Section 5.
5. Do not imply that WARENA, IKAI, a device, a model, or a software specification is operational without implementation evidence.
6. Do not imply that DON FELOH's authorship removes the need for distributed stewardship, succession, attribution, or review.
7. Preserve human dignity, privacy, provenance, and clear distinctions between concept, research, prototype, and live service.

## 8. Evidence and change discipline

This document records the brand constitution; it is not evidence that every associated project or claim is complete.

For material implementation or research claims, record the source and date, status, relevant repository or version, observed result, and limitations. Use explicit status labels such as `LIVE`, `PROTOTYPE`, `RESEARCH`, `CONCEPT`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `UNKNOWN`.

Changes to the three-pillar mapping, LOCS/DIGITAL public structure, or primary signature should be proposed through review rather than introduced as incidental copy edits.
