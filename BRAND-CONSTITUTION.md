# Brand Constitution — GRACE · GRIT · GLORY

**Status:** CANONICAL BRAND DIRECTION — pending review and merge  
**Scope:** Shared brand meaning, public signature, and audience-specific deployment  
**Applies to:** NAKEBUSEN, WARENA, and DON FELOH  
**Change control:** Review through a focused pull request; do not silently redefine this constitution in specialist repositories.

## 1. Unified signature

**NAKEBUSEN · WARENA · DON FELOH**  
**GRACE · GRIT · GLORY**  
**HERITAGE · BLACK EXCELLENCE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES**

The three entities remain distinct and are presented in this order. The umbrella statement is not a fourth pillar.

> Grace grounds us. Grit builds us. Glory expresses what we become.

## 2. The three pillars

### GRACE — NAKEBUSEN

**Beauty · Heritage · Craft · Human dignity**

NAKEBUSEN is the human-facing beauty, care, craft, livelihood, and heritage domain. Grace is excellence experienced through attentive service, cultural continuity, care, and dignity.

### GRIT — WARENA

**Technology · Infrastructure · Freedom · Capability**

WARENA is the computing, systems, infrastructure, and technical-capability domain. Grit is the disciplined work that makes capability durable: reproducibility, maintainability, repair, privacy, and freedom from unnecessary dependency.

### GLORY — DON FELOH

**Authorship · Embodiment · Creative vision · Achievement**

DON FELOH is the human authorship and creative-expression position. Glory means making work, practice, and possibility visible; it is not a claim of superiority or a substitute for evidence of achievement.

## 3. Constitutional relationship

The pillars are complementary, not interchangeable:

- **NAKEBUSEN / GRACE** grounds the ecosystem in human relationships, service, craft, and dignity.
- **WARENA / GRIT** builds and maintains the systems and capabilities that support the work.
- **DON FELOH / GLORY** authors and expresses creative work made possible through practice and capability.

The sequence is a brand and meaning-making framework. It does not by itself prove that any proposed technical system, cooperative, product, or research project has been implemented or validated.

The governing ecosystem boundary remains:

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.**

Specialist projects and creative identities do not independently imply authority to transact on behalf of NAKEBUSEN.

## 4. Audience-specific signature variants

The primary signature above is the default for broad brand use. The second line may be adapted to the context without changing the three-pillar mapping.

### Global African-diaspora, cultural, and creative contexts

**HERITAGE · BLACK EXCELLENCE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES**

Use where the intended audience and setting make the diasporic cultural register meaningful and appropriate.

### Kenyan cooperative, institutional, and public-sector contexts

**HERITAGE · SOVEREIGNTY · FUTURES**

A context-specific alternative is:

**HERITAGE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES · COOPERATIVE FUTURES**

Choose wording for the actual audience and document purpose. These are deployment variants, not replacements for the three pillars. Do not assume that any particular phrase is legally or institutionally preferred without checking the relevant context.

## 5. Cultural and historical grounding

The constitution is intended to support African self-representation, cultural continuity, technical self-determination, and futures shaped by African authorship. Relevant cultural, historical, philosophical, and institutional references should be documented with accurate citations and context.

Do not treat analogies between GRACE / GRIT / GLORY and a historical programme, philosophical tradition, or research framework as proof that those sources endorse this brand architecture. In particular, claims about UNESCO's General History of Africa, African philosophical concepts, sovereign-AI research, GitHub product releases, or economic forecasts belong in a sourced research dossier, not as uncited facts in this canonical statement.

## 6. Deployment rules

1. Keep the order **NAKEBUSEN · WARENA · DON FELOH** unless a documented, context-specific design decision requires otherwise.
2. Keep the mapping **GRACE—NAKEBUSEN; GRIT—WARENA; GLORY—DON FELOH** stable across official materials.
3. Treat **HERITAGE · BLACK EXCELLENCE · SOVEREIGN FUTURES** as the primary umbrella statement, with the audience-specific alternatives in Section 4.
4. Do not imply that WARENA, IKAI, a device, a model, or a software specification is operational without implementation evidence.
5. Do not imply that DON FELOH's authorship removes the need for distributed stewardship, succession, attribution, or review.
6. Preserve human dignity, privacy, provenance, and clear distinctions between concept, research, prototype, and live service.

## 7. Evidence and change discipline

This document records the brand constitution; it is not evidence that every associated project or claim is complete.

For material implementation or research claims, record the source and date, status, relevant repository or version, observed result, and limitations. Use explicit status labels such as `LIVE`, `PROTOTYPE`, `RESEARCH`, `CONCEPT`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `UNKNOWN`.

Changes to the three-pillar mapping or primary signature should be proposed through review rather than introduced as incidental copy edits.
