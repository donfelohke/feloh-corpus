## G. Contemporary political activity — evidence rules

For political movements, parties, officials, campaigns and fundraising:

1. Start with original party, movement, electoral, legal or regulatory documents.
2. Record the date, author, filing jurisdiction and exact scope.
3. Use reputable journalism to locate records and contextualise events.
4. Distinguish a tour announcement, filing, pledge, donation, receipt and audited expenditure.
5. Attribute claims about motives to the speaker or source.
6. Seek a response from the institution or person criticised where practicable.
7. Apply the same rules to government and opposition actors.

No single media report or political statement should be treated as conclusive evidence of disputed conduct.
