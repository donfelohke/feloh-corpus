## Research architecture

1. **Kenya — popular and constitutional sovereignty:** colonial institutional inheritance; constitutional government; devolution; land; public finance; civic space; elections.
2. **Africa — continental and economic sovereignty:** AU financing; Agenda 2063; regional integration; AfCFTA; resource governance; reparatory justice.
3. **Diaspora — citizenship and transnational participation:** electoral access; remittances; investment; political finance; knowledge exchange and civic organisation.

Research programmes are not practice domains, political parties or campaign organisations. The programme does not speak for a political movement or candidate.

## Evidence and editorial governance

This programme follows the FELOH Corpus [Evidence and Editorial Standards](../../EVIDENCE-AND-EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) and [Source Register and Research Intake](../source-register.md).

Use these claim labels:

- **VERIFIED** — supported by inspectable evidence appropriate to the claim.
- **CORROBORATED** — independently supported by multiple sources.
- **DISPUTED** — credible sources materially disagree.
- **UNVERIFIED** — evidence is insufficient.
- **INTERPRETATION** — reasoned analysis, with its evidence and logic exposed.
- **OPINION** — attributed viewpoint.
- **TESTIMONY** — contextualised first-person account.
- **PROPOSAL** — future intention, not an accomplished result.

A government or party statement verifies that the statement was made; it does not by itself verify the underlying claim. Do not turn allegations, forecasts, campaign statements or AI-generated imagery into historical fact.

## Editorial independence

The programme examines governments, opposition parties, political movements, public institutions, civil society, business interests and international actors using consistent standards. Edwin Sifuna, Linda Mwananchi and other political actors may be studied as subjects of contemporary political activity; inclusion does not imply endorsement, affiliation or representation.
