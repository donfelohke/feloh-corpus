# Cross-domain research update — real-time I/O and mirror systems

**Date:** 22 November 2026  
**Status:** Research routing and ownership record.

## Canonical responsibilities

- **IKAI / MIRROR:** reflective optics, two-way mirror M1 candidate evaluation, camera/display integration as a participant-facing reflective environment, and human-directed interaction.
- **WARENA / CAPABILITY:** native device interfaces, camera acquisition, display pipelines, local compute, real-time transport, synchronisation, timing, reproducibility and recovery.
- **GENJE / EXPRESSION:** authored movement, choreography, livecoding and creative interpretation; no sensor or motion-capture implementation is implied.
- **FELOH Corpus:** shared terminology, evidence, source provenance, decision history and cross-domain governance.

## Shared research model

**LIGHT IN → COMPUTE → LIGHT OUT**, with human/environment feedback. Separate reflection/transmission optics from camera capture, sensor input, compute and display output. A two-way / partially reflective mirror is the leading M1 candidate for initial evaluation, not a validated or procured component.

## Reuse and evidence rules

Evaluate existing scholarly work, standards, native APIs, open-source software and hardware designs before proposing new components. Preserve creators, upstream repositories, licences, revisions and local deltas. A fork is not an original invention; a research note is not a prototype; a passing CI job is not physical validation.

Measure real-time paths with explicit clocks and timestamp origins, throughput, queue depth, median/p95/p99/max latency, jitter, loss, synchronisation drift, power, thermal state and failure recovery. Do not publish performance claims without recorded test conditions and reproducible evidence.

## Privacy and participant authority

No face identification, biometric identity matching, person scoring, diagnosis, covert recording or hidden profiling. Capture must have a bounded purpose and explicit authorisation. Prefer local processing and make capture, retention, export, deletion and physical override visible.
