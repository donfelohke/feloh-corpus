## 2. Complete topic inventory

### 01 — Black history, identity and collective memory

African history and chronology; Black histories across Africa and the diaspora; civilisations, societies, kingdoms, states and communities; colonialism, enslavement, resistance and liberation; decolonisation and unfinished independence; consciousness, identity, belonging and representation; excellence, intellectual traditions and creative achievements; oral histories, biographies, family and community memory; living memory, testimony, silences and contested narratives; historiography and competing interpretations; political, economic, social and intellectual history; contemporary Black life and intergenerational experience.

### 02 — Philosophy, thought and intellectual traditions

African philosophy and epistemology; knowledge systems, truth, evidence and interpretation; indigenous knowledge and oral intellectual traditions; African humanism, ethics and social responsibility; freedom, justice, dignity, agency and personhood; power, knowledge, authority and legitimacy; decolonial and postcolonial critique; African political philosophy and social theory; intellectual biographies and schools of thought; Henry Odera Oruka and African philosophical traditions; comparative philosophy; thought, opinion, argument and evidence as distinct categories.

### 03 — Turkana, Ateker and indigenous knowledge

Turkana history, identity, language and heritage; Ateker peoples and comparative histories; migration, settlement, ancestry and historical geography; oral traditions, genealogies and community narratives; Turkana vocabulary, etymology, idioms and naming; knowledge transmission and community custodianship; land, pastoralism, ecology, livestock and livelihoods; local knowledge, memory and continuity; indigenous knowledge rights, consent and attribution; contemporary Turkana life; comparative linguistic and cultural research; local-first knowledge infrastructure and cultural self-determination.

### 04 — Culture, language and expression

African languages and linguistic diversity; Swahili, Sheng, English and multilingual communication; oral literature, poetry, proverbs, storytelling and performance; music, rhythm, dance and embodied knowledge; fashion, hair, adornment, aesthetics and self-expression; contemporary African creative movements; cultural exchange, appropriation, ownership and attribution; popular and digital culture; naming systems, symbolism and semiotics; cultural documentation, translation and contextual interpretation.

### 05 — People, lived experience and biography

Biographies and life histories; community testimony and oral-history interviews; family and intergenerational memory; education, work, entrepreneurship and livelihoods; migration, place, belonging and displacement; identity formation, self-representation and agency; everyday experience; creative practitioners and community contributors; consent, privacy, dignity and responsible representation; human authorship and preservation of individual contributions.

### 06 — Politics, society, law and political economy

Governance, institutions and public administration; political history and self-determination movements; citizenship, rights and civic participation; colonial and postcolonial political economies; land, property, resources and economic power; inequality, class, labour and livelihoods; education, public services and social infrastructure; law, intellectual property and cultural rights; media, public discourse and information power; community institutions and collective stewardship; African economic development and technological sovereignty.

### 07 — Science, technology and futures

African contributions to science, mathematics and engineering; technology histories and knowledge transfer; computing, communications and digital infrastructure; scientific literacy and evidence interpretation; appropriate and locally maintainable technology; AI and social implications; human-centred and assistive systems; technology sovereignty and local capability; climate, ecology and environmental futures; digital inclusion and technological inequality; emerging research and speculative futures (labelled as such).

### 08 — Research methodology and evidence standards

Source discovery and inventories; primary, secondary and tertiary sources; archives, manuscripts, books and articles; official statistics and datasets; oral testimony and community-generated evidence; annotated master bibliography; citation standards and bibliographic metadata; fact-checking, corroboration and claim verification; historical criticism, bias and limitations; quantitative and qualitative methods; comparative, historical, linguistic and interdisciplinary methods; surveys, interviews and sampling; research gaps and conflicting evidence; reproducible analysis and version-controlled research; separation of verified fact, interpretation, opinion, hypothesis and recommendation; research ethics, consent, privacy and community review.

### 09 — Documentation architecture and knowledge management

Master documentation tree and taxonomy; topic registers, glossaries and controlled vocabularies; research indexes and catalogues; entity records (person, place, event, concept); metadata schemas and identifiers; bibliographies and citation management; evidence registers and claims-to-sources mapping; data dictionaries; research and decision logs; archival structure and naming conventions; knowledge graphs and project relationships; completeness audits; publication versions and errata; long-term preservation and format sustainability; access controls, permissions and provenance.

### 10 — FELOH Corpus and Sun Burn — Black Musings

FELOH research, writing and creative corpus; *Sun Burn — Black Musings* source inventory; recovery from accessible conversations and files; original-source preservation and provenance; authored text vs quotations vs paraphrases vs external references; essays, notes, fragments, aphorisms and unfinished drafts; Black thought, cultural memory and philosophical reflections; taxonomy, tagging and thematic classification; cross-references across writing, research and projects; editorial review and publication readiness; authorship and IP boundaries; missing sources and unresolved provenance; controlled publication without importing unverified material.

### 11 — Research publishing and communications

Research website architecture and editorial strategy; web, technical and documentation copy; public summaries and detailed reports; annotated bibliographies and directories; explainers and educational material; evidence-led public scholarship; English-first global communication and Swahili localisation; language toggles; accessibility and plain language; SEO and metadata; canonical URLs and link integrity; corrections and version histories; research vs marketing communication; open access, licences and attribution.

### 12 — AI research and automation

AI-assisted discovery and synthesis; repository audits; research workflows; literature and bibliography support; structured extraction; claim verification and citation validation; human review of AI output; provenance, hallucination detection and uncertainty; reproducible prompts and procedures; automated documentation and link checks; agents, tools, integrations and permissions; automation safety and change control; limits of automated interpretation; integrity, privacy and IP; evidence-backed release processes.

### 13 — GitHub, repositories and project governance

Audit of related repositories; inventory, purpose, ownership and dependencies; README and contributor guidance; branch strategy, PRs and review order; reconciliation of overlapping website PRs; CI and deployment status; issues, milestones and releases; governance and distributed stewardship; onboarding and succession; commit history, tags and changelogs; GitHub Pages publishing; documentation and code review; testing, accessibility and link checks; asset inventories, licences and provenance; security, secrets and supply-chain risk; reviewable changes and verifiable completion evidence.

### 14 — Website design, development and QA

NAKEBUSEN site architecture and hierarchy; hero, beauty, technology and connect; journeys, navigation and CTAs; mobile-first responsive design; 320–760px and desktop verification; typography, spacing, contrast; top-right dark-mode toggle; accessibility and semantic HTML; contact and booking flows; canonical URL, Open Graph, text-only policy; SEO; performance and dependency reduction; offline-capable static HTML; Gallery linked not embedded; browser errors and release verification; web copy and language toggle; mock-up vs implementation vs live deployment evidence.

### 15 — NAKEBUSEN brand, beauty and loc-care business

Name, meaning and cultural context; Lodwar to Eldoret development; positioning and customer promise; loc care, retouch, styling; Sisterlocs reties; artificial loc installation; service definitions, prices and booking; customer experience and trust; salon operations; natural loc craft; communication, photography and consent; marketing and referrals; quality and capacity; pricing, margins and sustainability; beauty vs training/products; distinction between NAKEBUSEN beauty and NAKEBUSEN Digital.

### 16 — Market research, TAM, SAM and SOM

TAM, SAM, SOM; Eldoret / Uasin Gishu beachhead; Kenya-wide and regional expansion; loc-care counts and frequency; competitor mapping; segments and willingness to pay; digital-services market; school/SME/office IT demand; product and training markets; interviews and surveys; sizing from population and spending data; scenarios and sensitivity; acquisition, conversion and retention; capacity and obtainable revenue; source dates, confidence and reproducibility.

**Note:** Population context (e.g. Eldoret 2019 census figures) is public context only — not proof of local demand at listed prices.

### 17 — WARENA brand and business model

WARENA as systems practice; NAKEBUSEN Digital relationship; technology services and engineering; Freedom Is a System; local-first and libre-first; sovereign systems offline-capable; Linux administration; infrastructure audits and support contracts; client profiles; productised services and recurring revenue; pricing and capacity; technical docs and catalogues; continuity and recovery; differentiation; long-term development and stewardship.

### 18 — GNU/Linux, free software and sovereign infrastructure

GNU/Linux administration; FOSS; reproducible and declarative systems; local-first and offline architectures; Guix / RDE where documented; Btrfs, SQLite, Git, Restic; backup, recovery and portability; lightweight desktop; network design; local servers and caches; reproducible research environments; hardware selection and repairability; software freedom and security; reduced cloud dependence; runbooks.

### 19 — Networks, hardware and technical services

Edge hardware concepts (e.g. H68K, RK3568) where researched; x86 routers and OPNsense/pfSense; LAN, Wi-Fi, VLANs, mesh; multi-ISP failover; WireGuard/IPsec; QoS; hotspot concepts; offline caching; PXE and lab deployment; school/SME networks; firewalling and security; monitoring and remote support; BOM and lifecycle costs; storage and DR; audits and pricing; offline resilience.

### 20 — IKAI: reflective computing

### 21 — GENJE, GENJE-NESS, Genge and embodied expression

### 24 — Entrepreneurship, finance and digital economies

## 6. Maintenance

Treat this as the **master subject index**, not evidence that every topic is researched.

Each topic should eventually carry: stable ID and title; scope and research questions; status (proposed / collecting / researching / reviewed / published / archived); sources and claim-level citations; verified findings vs interpretations vs opinions vs recommendations; missing evidence; responsible contributor; last-reviewed date; next action; related paths.

**Canonical principle:** one shared research and documentation system · multiple specialised projects · traceable sources · independently reviewable changes.

This register consolidates known programme topics. It is **not** a verified inventory of every file, conversation, repository or unpublished work.
