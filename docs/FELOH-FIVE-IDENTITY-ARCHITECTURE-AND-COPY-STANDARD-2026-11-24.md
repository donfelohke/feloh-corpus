# FELOH Five-Identity Architecture & Copy Standard

**Date:** 24 November 2026  
**Status:** Founder-directed identity and copy revision  
**Scope:** Shared identity language for GitHub, public website, project READMEs and publications  
**Human author:** Felix Lwova · Feloh Warena · Don Feloh · [@donfelohke](https://github.com/donfelohke)

## 1. Governing decision

The former five-part verb sentence is retired from public-facing copy and repository documentation. It is replaced by a noun-led identity signature. The five identities are distinct domains connected by one human author and a shared body of work.

## 2. Canonical five-identity signature

**FELOH** — Authorship · Intellectual work · Creative direction  
**NAKEBUSEN** — Beauty · Heritage · Craft · Enterprise  
**IKAI** — Reflective computing · Perception · Memory · Human experience  
**WARENA** — Libre technology · Infrastructure · Systems engineering · Digital sovereignty  
**GENJE** — Music · Movement · Performance · Creative expression

Use this signature as a compact identity index. It is not a sequence, process model, corporate ownership chart or claim that every domain is a released product.

## 3. Identity definitions

### FELOH — AUTHORSHIP

The human author, founder, intellectual origin and accountable creative direction across the work. Public role direction: Founder · CTO · Loctician · Creative Technologist. FELOH is not a fifth practice business or transaction interface.

### NAKEBUSEN — ROOT

Beauty, loc care, heritage, craft, dignity and enterprise. NAKEBUSEN is the primary customer-facing enterprise and the only commercial transaction interface. **ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.**

### IKAI — REFLECTION

Reflective computing, perception, memory, mirror systems and human experience. IKAI is a research domain and work in progress, not a released product. Its governing principle is **A MIRROR, NOT A JUDGE**. Human authority, consent, local control and privacy remain mandatory.

### WARENA — CAPABILITY

Libre-first technology, GNU/Linux, systems engineering, infrastructure, networking, reproducibility and digital sovereignty. WARENA's maxim remains **FREEDOM IS A SYSTEM**. WARENA provides technical capability; it is not a competing checkout or a parent company over the other identities.

### GENJE — EXPRESSION

Genge-rooted music, livecoding, sound, movement, choreography, performance and creative expression. GENJE is an independent creative practice. **GENJE ZA WARENA** describes an authored creative relationship and shared signature, not a merger, subsidiary or product claim.

## 4. GRACE · GRIT · GLORY

The three-part value architecture remains unchanged:

- **GRACE — NAKEBUSEN:** Beauty · Heritage · Craft · Dignity.
- **GRIT — WARENA:** Freedom · Engineering · Resilience · Infrastructure.
- **GLORY — DON FELOH:** Authorship · Expression · Creativity · Becoming.

GENJE retains its own creative identity and expression. IKAI retains its own research identity. Neither should be forced into a misleading fourth or fifth value pillar.

## 5. Relationship and governance

- FELOH is the human author and accountable decision-maker.
- NAKEBUSEN is the primary enterprise and sole customer-facing transaction point.
- WARENA is a distinct technical capability practice.
- IKAI is reflective-computing research.
- GENJE is creative and embodied practice.
- FELOH Corpus is the shared constitutional, evidence and provenance record.
- Gallery is an asset-rights, consent, attribution and approval repository.
- Supporting software forks remain implementation repositories with upstream attribution.

## 6. Copy rules

1. Use the noun-led signature; do not restore the retired verb sentence.
2. Describe each identity with concrete disciplines, outputs, services or evidence.
3. Keep research, concepts, prototypes, deployed systems and commercial services distinct.
4. Do not imply that a research interest is a customer-ready product.
5. Keep the NAKEBUSEN commercial boundary explicit.
6. Preserve upstream authorship, licence obligations, attribution and project history.
7. Prefer concise domain language over abstract slogans or inflated skill inventories.
8. Use **GRACE · GRIT · GLORY** as the shared ethos, not as a substitute for the five identity descriptions.

## 7. Application

Apply this standard to the GitHub profile, public website, repository descriptions and READMEs, cross-project indexes, project introductions and future publications. Specialist repositories remain authoritative for their own technical, service, research, creative, rights and operational facts.
