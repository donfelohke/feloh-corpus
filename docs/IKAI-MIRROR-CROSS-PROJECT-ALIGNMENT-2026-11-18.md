## Purpose

Record the ecosystem boundary and research responsibilities arising from IKAI's dedicated mirror-technology track. The IKAI repository owns the detailed mirror research note, optical analysis, scholarly bibliography, M1 proposal and module definitions. This corpus records shared meaning, provenance and cross-project boundaries; it does not duplicate the specialist note.

## Shared proposition

IKAI investigates a computationally augmented reflective environment in which physical reflection remains meaningful. The mirror is an interface for participant-directed reflection, not an authority that judges, diagnoses, scores or profiles a person.

The research distinguishes three layers:

- **Physical self:** the embodied person and actual optical reflection.
- **Computational self:** information and transformations introduced by the system.
- **Represented self:** any visual, spatial or recorded representation presented by the interface.

These layers must remain legible and must not be conflated.

## Research architecture relationship

The complementary IKAI tracks are:

- **Mirror:** stationary reflective environment and the proposed M1 proof-of-concept.
- **Glass:** wearable voice and visual-AR interaction research.
- **Space:** spatial, room-scale and immersive interaction research.

These are complementary research surfaces, not three released products. Integration should be assessed at the architecture and interface-contract level before selecting a single device or runtime.

## M1 status and proof discipline

The IKAI specialist note proposes a stationary Mirror M1 and four proof criteria. Those criteria remain research gates until the specialist record supplies measurable thresholds, test conditions, results and limitations. A documented proposal, component recommendation or scholarly reference is not proof of a working prototype.

No procurement, implementation, deployment, clinical efficacy, beauty-service outcome or commercial availability is asserted here.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- No emotion recognition or affective inference as a default capability.
- No person scoring, identity profiling, diagnosis or covert surveillance.
- No mandatory cloud account or cloud processing as the starting architecture.
- Minimise data, state the purpose, prefer local processing and make retention and deletion understandable.
- Keep participant agency, physical reflection and the distinction between actual and computed representations explicit.
- Evaluate sensors only where a defined interaction requirement justifies them.

## Technology-policy precedence

WARENA's current native-first, libre-first and local-first technology policy governs infrastructure evaluation. JavaScript and web technologies are not approved as IKAI application/runtime architecture. Candidate APIs and frameworks remain candidates until source, licence, hardware support, reproducibility, data flows and recovery are qualified.
