# Cross-Domain Knowledge Map

**Status:** PROPOSAL — scope and repository routing  
**Review date:** 2026-10-26  
**Public identity:** NAKEBUSEN LOCS • DIGITAL · Powered by WARENA · @donfelohke

This map routes knowledge to the right home. It is not evidence that every listed service, system, research project, or workflow is operational.

## Domain register

| Domain | Core questions | Canonical / working home | Evidence expected |
|---|---|---|---|

| NAKEBUSEN LOCS | What services are offered? What method, care, price, and aftercare are approved? | [NAKEBUSEN](https://github.com/donfelohke/NAKEBUSEN) | Approved catalogue, dated pricing, procedure notes, customer-safe operating records |
| NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL | What technology service is being requested, scoped, delivered, and handed over? | [NAKEBUSEN](https://github.com/donfelohke/NAKEBUSEN) for public service/operations; [WARENA](https://github.com/donfelohke/WARENA) for technical implementation | Scope, assumptions, quote/approval record without private client data, test and handover evidence |

| GENJE / FELOH creative practice | How are code, sound, movement, Genge, and performance authored and documented? | [GENJE](https://github.com/donfelohke/Genje) | Source/artifact, date, collaborators, attribution, licence, setup and reproduction notes |

## Shared conceptual relationships

- **FELOH generates:** authorship, direction, and expression.
- **NAKEBUSEN grounds:** care, craft, livelihood, and the public service relationship.
- **WARENA enables:** systems, infrastructure, and technical capability.
- **IKAI reflects:** research into reflective compute; research status must remain explicit.
- **GENJE embodies:** culture, code, sound, movement, and performance.

The relationship is a conceptual framework, not implementation evidence.

## Routing rules

1. Put shared definitions and cross-domain policy in the FELOH Corpus.
2. Put operational beauty procedures and approved service facts in NAKEBUSEN.
3. Put technical source code, configurations, and reproducibility evidence in WARENA.
4. Keep IKAI protocols and experiments in IKAI; do not describe work in progress as a released product.
5. Put creative source, performance notes, and attribution in GENJE.
6. Put assets and rights metadata in Gallery; file presence alone does not grant permission to publish.
7. Keep public web copy concise and consistent with the approved source records.
8. Do not copy a large canonical document into multiple repositories. Link to it and maintain a small local summary when needed.
9. Do not store private client/customer records, secrets, credentials, or payment references in public repositories.
10. If ownership, current status, or source-of-record is uncertain, label it UNKNOWN and open a decision rather than guessing.

## Cross-domain work record

For a task spanning multiple domains, record:
- Question and expected outcome
- Lead domain and affected domains
- Canonical sources reviewed
- Dependencies and assumptions
- Evidence and unresolved questions
- Files/repositories changed
- Tests and observations completed
- Review/approval state
- Follow-up owner or next action, if assigned

## Current limitation

This map is a proposed routing standard. Repository links are known; individual repository maturity, service availability, and completeness must be checked independently before a claim is made about current operations.
