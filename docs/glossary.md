## Status labels

- **LIVE** — currently operating or in active use, with evidence appropriate to the claim.
- **PROTOTYPE** — an implementation exists but is incomplete or experimental.
- **RESEARCH** — investigation or evaluation is underway; no production claim implied.
- **CONCEPT** — an idea or proposed design without verified implementation evidence.
- **HISTORICAL** — retained to preserve the record of a prior state or decision.
- **SUPERSEDED** — replaced by a newer accepted position; preserve links to the replacement.
- **UNKNOWN** — available evidence is insufficient to classify the claim confidently.

Use **LIVE** for this repository's documentation only when referring to documents that are actually present and committed. Do not infer that all projects or services described by the corpus are live.
