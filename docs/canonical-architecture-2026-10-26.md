## 1. Governing principles

- **The root becomes the runtime.**
- **AI suggests. The human decides.**
- Human authorship, dignity, cultural context, local capability, reproducibility, privacy, recoverability and self-determination take precedence over technology for its own sake.
- Distinguish documented facts, owner-supplied information, interpretation, proposals, and implemented/tested behavior.
- A repository, document, specification or commit is not by itself evidence that a system is deployed or works.

## 2. Canonical enterprise architecture

**FELOH → NAKEBUSEN → NAKEBUSEN LOCS + NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL**

- **FELOH:** human author and integrated practice spanning physical care, craft, culture, computation, movement research, creative work and infrastructure.
- **NAKEBUSEN:** parent enterprise, commercial accountability and customer-facing transaction interface.
- **NAKEBUSEN LOCS:** loc craft, maintenance, retouching, crochet retwist and styling, artificial loc installation, Sisterlocks maintenance where offered, and other beauty services only when actually available.
- **NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL:** digital services and technical operations.
- **WARENA:** technical identity of NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL.
- **WARENA Systems:** systems engineering, GNU/Linux, reproducible environments, infrastructure, networking, storage, recovery and technical operations.
- **IKAI Mirror / IKAI REFLECTIVE (IR-01):** reflective-computing research project.
- **GENJE ZA WARENA:** cultural identity and motivating idea; the current naming of NIA. It is not a separate commercial division.
- **DadaNet:** related network/project concept; scope and relationship to WARENA remain unresolved.
- **Gallery:** curated assets, provenance, rights and approval.
- **@donfelohke / #HisGenjeness:** public creative identity and human authorship.

**Commercial rule: ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.** WARENA, IKAI and GENJE must not compete with NAKEBUSEN for customer booking/checkout attention. An enquiry is not a booking.

## 3. GENJE ZA WARENA and NIA

Current identity line: **GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE.**  
Role: the name, cultural identity and motivating idea. NIA remains a functional question about why, whom, intention, direction, action, evidence and continuity.

Working brand interpretations supplied by the author:
- *Genje*: associations proposed include kali, great, noma, fire and formidable energy.
- *Za*: intended as by/of/belonging to, subject to grammatical and contextual validation.
- *Warena*: intended brand meaning “The Great, The Powerful.”
- *Genjeness*: creative identity/coinage associated with #HisGenjeness.

These are **brand interpretations and research leads, not established universal dictionary definitions**. Research must separate attested usage, contextual interpretation and intentional brand meaning. Verify language, dialect, source, date and context; consider competing interpretations and avoid unsupported etymological claims.

## 9. Evidence and maturity labels

Record the chain **CLAIM → DOCUMENT → IMPLEMENTATION → OBSERVATION → RECORD → ATTESTATION** when relevant. Never convert a proposal into a deployment claim by repetition.
