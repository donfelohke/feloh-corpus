## 1. Executive proposition

FELOH is a human-authored, African-rooted practice connecting physical craft, enterprise, technical capability, reflective research and creative expression. It is not a conventional holding company, a fifth product domain or a collection of unrelated personal projects.

Its organising proposition is:

**ONE CORPUS → MANY VIEWS → ONE PRACTICE.**

The person remains the author and decision-maker. Technology should extend human capability without silently acquiring authority over identity, interpretation or consequential action. Enterprise should sustain practical work without turning every research and creative activity into a sales proposition. Cultural practice should be contemporary without being detached from its context.

This document is a strategic case and a programme for substantiation. It is not proof that every proposed system, service, research prototype or institutional arrangement is operational.

## 2. The problem and the response

The practice responds to five connected problems:

1. **Loss of human authorship:** opaque systems can mediate identity, knowledge and action without making their assumptions or authority visible.
2. **Dependence without control:** convenience can conceal weak portability, repairability, recoverability and exit options.
3. **Fragmented institutional memory:** decisions spread across conversations, files, repositories and drafts can lose provenance or contradict one another.
4. **Cultural disconnection:** heritage can be reduced to decoration, while contemporary technical work can be detached from lived cultural context.
5. **Vision without operational proof:** broad inventories and ambitious language can be mistaken for implemented capability.

FELOH's response is a governed practice with distinct responsibilities, a shared source of record, explicit commercial boundaries and evidence-based maturity labels.

## 4. The philosophical case

The practice is governed by the following commitments:

- **Human authorship:** AI and automation may assist, but the human retains decision rights.
- **Capability without capture:** systems should increase capability while preserving inspection, portability and exit.
- **Observation is not truth:** sensor readings, logs, model outputs, testimony and memory are contextual evidence, not automatic ground truth.
- **Capability is not permission:** technical ability does not confer authority to act.
- **Local-first continuity:** prefer local processing and portable records where practical; justify external dependencies.
- **Reversibility:** consequential changes require review, provenance and a recovery path.
- **Cultural continuity:** language, place, community and lived practice are sources of meaning, not decorative assets.
- **Evidence before claims:** distinguish interpretation, proposal, research, prototype, tested implementation and deployment.

These are governing principles, not claims that every current component already satisfies them.

## 5. Domain cases

### 5.1 NAKEBUSEN — ROOT

NAKEBUSEN grounds the wider practice in loc care, craft, dignity, community and commercial service. It is the only live commercial enterprise and the customer-facing transaction interface.

The business case must be demonstrated through service delivery, customer experience, transparent pricing, repeat relationships, costs, cash flow and reliable operations. Published service details and starting prices must be governed by the current service catalogue and operations records.

The wider digital offer is scoped through NAKEBUSEN, with WARENA supplying technical capability. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Research concepts are not customer-ready services.

### 5.2 WARENA — CAPABILITY

WARENA develops systems that people can understand, inspect, maintain, recover and govern. Its engineering programme favours libre-first software, open standards, local-first operation, reproducible configuration, explicit dependencies and tested recovery.

GNU Guix and RDE are preferred foundations in the current research direction. The minimum implementation should be small and tied to a named target. A technology inventory is not a deployment record.

For every implementation claim, record the target, source revision, versions, configuration, test conditions, observed result, limitations and date.

### 5.3 IKAI — MIRROR

IKAI investigates reflective computing and human-directed interaction in physical environments. Its research separates sensing, acquisition, computation, rendering, timing, calibration, interpretation and validation.

For distributed spatial computing, the key problems include device autonomy, local estimation, authenticated exchange, network partitions, tracking loss, clock drift, map disagreement and explicit reconciliation.

The system must represent uncertainty and preserve participant authority. No face identification, biometric matching, ranking, diagnosis, covert recording or hidden profiling.

IKAI remains research / work in progress until an integrated prototype and evaluation demonstrate otherwise.

### 5.4 GENJE — EXPRESSION

GENJE connects Genge-rooted sound and aesthetics, livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, rhythm, movement, choreography, notation and choreohacking.

It treats the body and creative practitioner as authors, not as generic data sources. Tools such as TidalCycles, SuperCollider and Hydra are candidates in an explored practice; their mention does not establish installation, a released work or a reproducible performance environment.

“The Fire of the Formidable” is an adopted creative interpretation, not a universal etymology.

## 6. The corpus as institutional memory

The FELOH Corpus is the shared source of record for:

- constitutional identity and domain boundaries;
- owner-confirmed decisions and decision history;
- controlled terminology and naming notes;
- source register, citations and provenance;
- evidence and maturity classifications;
- cross-repository indexes and publication controls;
- superseded records and reasons for change.

Specialist repositories own their detailed research, practice and implementation records. The website is an audience-facing view, not the complete knowledge base.

A statement is not canonical merely because it appears in a file on the default branch. Authority requires identifiable authorship, provenance, review and explicit acceptance.

The R2 edition is not canonical merely because it is newer or longer. The original source lineage, owner approval and claim-level differences require reconciliation.

## 7. Evidence and maturity controls

Every material claim should be assigned an evidence class:

- **VERIFIED** — directly demonstrated with traceable evidence.
- **CORROBORATED** — supported by independent compatible sources.
- **DISPUTED** — credible sources or interpretations conflict.
- **UNVERIFIED** — not yet adequately substantiated.
- **INTERPRETATION** — an authored reading or synthesis.
- **OPINION** — a stated position or judgement.
- **TESTIMONY** — attributed personal or community account.
- **PROPOSAL** — a future intention or design.

Every project or capability should also carry a maturity label:

**LIVE · PROTOTYPE · RESEARCH · CONCEPT · HISTORICAL · SUPERSEDED · UNKNOWN**

For technical systems, distinguish at minimum:

**DOCUMENTED → CONFIGURED → BUILT → TESTED → VALIDATED → DEPLOYED → PRODUCTION-VERIFIED**

Do not infer one status from another. A repository proves source presence, not deployment. A successful build does not prove a validated system. A passing test does not establish sustained production operation.

## 8. Objections and responses

### Is FELOH too broad?

It becomes too broad if every idea is treated as an active deliverable. Keep the constitutional architecture broad but the implementation baseline narrow. Prioritise NAKEBUSEN operations and one validated WARENA target while advancing IKAI and GENJE through bounded research and creative outputs.

### Is this only a personal brand?

It would be if the work stopped at naming, slogans and visual identity. The case must be demonstrated through real service delivery, working technical artefacts, documented methods, original creative outputs and evidence of outcomes.

### Is the technical programme over-engineered?

It could be. Use existing standards and projects before building new systems. Add dependencies only where a requirement and acceptance test justify them.

### Does cultural framing replace technical rigour?

No. Cultural grounding explains purpose and context; it does not replace evidence, testing, attribution, consent or engineering quality.

### Can the domains be sustainable?

They need not all monetise or mature at the same rate. NAKEBUSEN is the commercial foundation. WARENA develops practical capability. IKAI and GENJE advance as research and creative practices with defined outputs.

## 10. Execution programme

1. **Source of truth:** recover missing exports and registers; reconcile the master document lineages; establish source IDs and decision authority.
2. **NAKEBUSEN operations:** maintain an accurate service catalogue, customer journey, booking disclosure, payment safeguards and business records.
3. **WARENA baseline:** select one target, establish a minimal Guix/RDE configuration, pin sources, test hardware, and demonstrate backup restoration.
4. **IKAI integration prototype:** define a bounded experiment, interfaces, timing and uncertainty model, consent requirements and acceptance tests.
5. **GENJE practice outputs:** document original sound, movement and livecoding work with credits, methods and asset provenance.
6. **Evidence-led publication:** publish audience-specific views from governed source records; do not turn proposals into operational claims.

No purchase, deployment, external commitment or consequential system action is implied by this strategy.

## 11. Public position

FELOH is a practice of building continuity between the human being, the crown, the community, the creative act, the machine and the systems that sustain them.

NAKEBUSEN grounds it in physical care and enterprise. WARENA develops technical capability. IKAI investigates reflection without judgement. GENJE gives it sound, movement and creative expression. FELOH maintains authorship, memory and governance.

The ambition is not to make technology sovereign over people. It is to make the systems people depend on more understandable, recoverable, accountable and governable.
