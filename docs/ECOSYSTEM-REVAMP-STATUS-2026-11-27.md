## 5. Research and evidence standards

- Record source, author/organisation, publication date, access date, finding, relevance, limitation and resulting decision.
- Prefer primary standards, original project documentation, peer-reviewed scholarship and direct upstream source over unsourced summaries.
- Separate evidence from interpretation and design proposals.
- Record exact versions, test fixtures, commands, environments and outcomes for technical validation.
- Distinguish **RESEARCH**, **CONCEPT**, **PROTOTYPE**, **IMPLEMENTATION**, **VALIDATION** and **DEPLOYMENT**.
- A merged PR is not a deployment. A successful build is not runtime validation. A research note is not an implemented capability.
- Preserve upstream licences, creator credit, provenance and rights. Reuse and interoperate before inventing.
