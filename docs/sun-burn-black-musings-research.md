## Recommendation

Treat the collection as a **creative-work archive plus a separately maintained intellectual-history index**, within the existing corpus for now. Do not create a separate repository until an inventory demonstrates a practical need for independent permissions, publication lifecycle, or scale.

Keep these layers distinct:

1. **Source work** — the original wording and source reference.
2. **Editorial edition** — corrections, formatting, transcription, or a selected version, with changes recorded.
3. **Author-stated position** — a dated claim or belief, attributed to the author's own words.
4. **Research annotation** — external sources, questions, themes, and counterarguments.
5. **AI-assisted synthesis** — a derivative interpretation that cites its sources and is never represented as the author's original statement.

## Required next inventory

For each discovered source, record:

- stable ID and source location;
- original title and exact text/media reference;
- author/attribution as supported by the source;
- creation/publication dates, or `UNKNOWN`;
- provenance and any known revision history;
- privacy/publication status;
- whether the record is original, edited, transcribed, or synthesized;
- unresolved questions and next action.

Do not move private material into a public repository without explicit publication approval. Do not infer sensitive personal or psychological attributes from creative writing.

## Acceptance criteria for the next phase

- The author confirms the intended meaning and scope of the collection.
- The source inventory lists searched locations and any coverage gaps.
- Original works are preserved without silent rewriting.
- Editorial notes and AI-assisted analysis are clearly labeled.
- Public/private status is explicit before publication.
- Relative links and metadata are validated.
- Any imported work has a traceable source reference.
