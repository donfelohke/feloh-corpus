## 7. Visual system

**Direction:** Liquid Glass Terminal × Editorial Hybrid.

- Forest green: WARENA and shared technical surfaces.
- Ivory: editorial reading surfaces.
- Gold / ochre: GRACE and heritage accents.
- Terracotta: embodied warmth and expressive accents.
- Near-black: terminal and contrast surfaces.
- Cormorant Garamond: editorial display typography.
- Inter: interface and body typography.
- JetBrains Mono: technical labels and metadata.

Glass is a material treatment, not a substitute for hierarchy or contrast. Use deliberate depth, restrained reflections, fine borders, negative space and strong typographic structure. Avoid generic luxury templates, decorative cultural shorthand, unsupported imagery, gratuitous glitch and visual clutter.

The public website remains text-first and native HTML/CSS. Do not introduce JavaScript runtime dependencies, remote fonts, CDNs, analytics or trackers. Gallery assets are not to be embedded in the public site under the current publication policy.
