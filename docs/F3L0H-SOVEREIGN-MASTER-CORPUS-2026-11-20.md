# PART I — CONSTITUTION

## 1. Purpose

F3L0H is a human-centred, African-rooted practice connecting physical craft, reflective computing, creative expression, sovereign infrastructure and community knowledge.

Its purpose is to develop and preserve knowledge, practices, technologies and systems that extend human capability without transferring human authority to machines, corporations or centralised platforms.

The corpus is the authoritative knowledge foundation for the practice. It is not a collection of unrelated startups, products or autonomous commercial entities.
