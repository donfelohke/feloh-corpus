# Repository upgrade plan — 2026-11-21

**Repository:** `feloh-corpus`  
**Primary role:** Shared constitutional canon, governance, evidence, provenance and cross-domain indexes.  
**Status:** Upgrade plan; not a claim that source consolidation is complete.

## Governing rule

The Corpus is the source of record for shared canon. Specialist repositories retain their detailed research, engineering, creative and enterprise records. Do not duplicate specialist documents wholesale or silently rewrite historical material.

## Priority workstreams

1. **Exact-source completion:** import and reconcile the complete F3L0H-PRESENT-001 edition (Parts I–XV, §§1–60) and its folder tree from the exact source. Keep the 2026-11-20 Master Corpus identified as the accepted constitutional spine, not a verbatim complete transcription until verified.
2. **Provenance:** record source identity, author/creator, date, version, rights, transformation history and confidence for imported material.
3. **Decision ledger:** distinguish adopted canon, current policy, proposal, research hypothesis, superseded wording and unresolved question.
4. **Cross-repository consistency:** maintain a dated register for NAKEBUSEN, WARENA, IKAI, GENJE, Gallery, website and profile; identify the authoritative owner for every shared fact.
5. **Evidence discipline:** separate LIVE, PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH, CONCEPT, HISTORICAL, SUPERSEDED and UNKNOWN. A commit, note or roadmap is not proof of deployment.
6. **Research quality:** require primary sources where possible, dated citations, limitations, conflicting evidence and a reuse-before-inventing review.
7. **Governance:** retain human authorship and final approval; AI-assisted changes must disclose sources, transformations and review status.
