## 6. Evidence and maturity vocabulary

Use these terms consistently:

- **CANONICAL / APPROVED:** an accepted identity, policy or owner-approved direction.
- **RESEARCH:** investigation or evidence collection; not implementation.
- **PROPOSED:** a possible approach not yet accepted as a decision.
- **PROTOTYPE:** an artefact exists; scope and limitations must be stated.
- **IMPLEMENTED:** functionality exists in a specified revision.
- **VERIFIED:** tested against explicit criteria with recorded evidence.
- **DEPLOYED:** operating in a stated environment; deployment evidence is required.
- **UNKNOWN / PENDING:** evidence or a decision is absent.

A document, design brief, successful compile or research note does not by itself prove implementation, validation, deployment, certification, compliance or commercial availability.
