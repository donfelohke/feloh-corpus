## IKAI engineering direction

IKAI has two coordinated but separately specified goals:

1. **Software:** a native, local-first reflective-computing environment coordinating optical acquisition, device control, local computation/state, display output, participant-directed interaction, privacy and data lifecycle.
2. **Hardware:** an M1 reflective-mirror prototype validating passive mirror utility, optical transmission/reflectivity, display visibility, controlled illumination, mechanical/thermal/electrical safety, local compute integration and optional participant-controlled sensing.

**REFLECTION±** describes a system loop: **LIGHT IN → COMPUTE → LIGHT OUT**, with human/environment feedback. The ± denotes reciprocal input/output and feedback; it does not imply that a passive mirror itself computes. Camera/sensor capture must be explicit, optional and revocable. No facial identification, beauty scoring, emotion inference, covert recording, diagnosis or mandatory cloud dependency. The mirror remains useful when compute is off.

V4L2, DMA-BUF, DRM/KMS, GStreamer and PipeWire are candidate interfaces/components where justified, not claims of completed integration. OpenXR is conditional on a real XR requirement. Separate software milestones from optical/mechanical prototype milestones and report measurements, calibration, uncertainty and limitations.
