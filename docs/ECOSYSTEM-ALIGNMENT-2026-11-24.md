## Evidence and maturity discipline

- A repository file proves that the file exists on the referenced branch, not that a system is deployed.
- Research is not a prototype; a prototype is not a release.
- A commit is not proof of testing, operational availability, commercial readiness or independent reproducibility.
- Linguistic and historical claims must retain attribution, language/dialect, evidence and uncertainty. Creator-defined meanings must be labelled as such.
- Prices, services, booking terms and contact routes remain owned by NAKEBUSEN's current service and operations records.
- Rights, consent and asset approval remain owned by Gallery's asset-level records.

## Documentation change policy

Specialist repositories own detailed technical, commercial, creative and research records. The Corpus owns shared definitions and cross-project navigation. Avoid duplicating dossiers; link to canonical specialist records and record provenance when a shared definition changes.

Substantive changes should use focused branches and pull requests. Human review remains authoritative. Do not silently rewrite historical records or claim implementation from documentation alone.
