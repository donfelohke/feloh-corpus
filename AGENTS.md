# FELOH Corpus contributor and agent instructions

## Canonical role
The corpus is the source of record for shared terminology, governance, decisions, evidence, provenance, and status. Specialist repositories should link to canonical definitions rather than silently create competing versions.

Read `README.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `EVIDENCE-AND-PROVENANCE-METHOD.md`, and the relevant domain skill before proposing changes.

## Evidence and authorship
- Preserve original wording, context, source references, dates, and historical status.
- Distinguish verified fact, attributed statement, interpretation, proposal, aspiration, unknown, and superseded material.
- Do not invent missing creative works, quotes, opinions, psychological claims, citations, or source records.
- Preserve privacy; do not import private messages, customer data, credentials, or sensitive personal details into public records without an explicit, appropriate basis.
- A conversation-derived skill or specification is not automatically installed, tested, or implemented.

## Change control
- Use focused branches and pull requests for canonical changes.
- Record why a decision changed, the evidence supporting it, what remains disputed or unknown, and affected downstream repositories.
- Do not rewrite historical records silently or treat a merged document as proof of operational implementation.
- Human authorship and approval remain authoritative; automation proposes and validates, not owns canon.
